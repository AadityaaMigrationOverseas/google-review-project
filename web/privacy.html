Privacy notice

How Stellar Dental Clinic handles the information you share through this feedback portal.

What we collect

  • Feedback: your star rating, your comments, the time you submitted them, and which clinic link you used (for example, a WhatsApp or QR link).
  • Private complaints: your first name, surname, email address, phone number, rating and the details you provide.
  • Security data: a one-way, pseudonymous fingerprint of your IP address, used only to prevent spam and abuse. We do not store your raw IP address.

How we use it

  • To understand and improve the care we provide.
  • Your comments are analysed automatically on our own server to help staff spot trends (for example, positive or negative experiences). This analysis never changes what you see or what options you are offered, and it is never shared outside the clinic.
  • Complaints are sent securely to Dr. Dhruvi Shah so she can follow up with you personally.

What we never do

  • We never post anything on Google or any other site on your behalf. Writing a Google review is entirely optional and is something you do yourself, in your own words.
  • We never treat patients differently based on what they write.
  • We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Who can see it

Only authorised clinic staff can see feedback, and only senior staff can see complaint contact details. Every access to complaint contact details is logged.

How long we keep it

We keep feedback and complaints only for the period set out in the clinic's records policy, in line with our legal and professional obligations, after which they are permanently deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you have shared by contacting the clinic. Please avoid including medical details you would prefer to keep private.
